      About

      Every point is a real star at its catalogued 3D position: the Yale Bright Star Catalogue (names, colours, brightness of 8,401 naked-eye stars) with distances from Gaia DR3 and Hipparcos via AT-HYG, plus the Gaia Catalogue of Nearby Stars for the faint neighbours within 326 light-years. Brightness follows the inverse-square law from wherever you are. Milky Way glow: NASA/Goddard SVS Deep Star Maps 2020 (Gaia DR2: ESA/Gaia/DPAC). Constellation figures: d3-celestial by Olaf Frohn. Planet and moon orbits: JPL approximate Keplerian elements and mean satellite elements, Meeus's lunar theory, IAU rotation models.
